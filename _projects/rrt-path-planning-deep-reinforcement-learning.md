This project investigated ways to improve initial path-planning solutions from Rapidly-exploring Random Trees by letting previous sampling experience inform future candidate states.

## Tools

- RRT
- RRT*
- DeRRT*
- Recurrent neural networks
- Hidden Markov Models

## Highlights

- Modified RRT sampling so future states could be conditioned on past sampling behavior.
- Developed baseline learned-experience systems using Hidden Markov Models and recurrent neural networks.
- Compared learned sampling behavior against conventional sampling-based planning approaches.
- Focused on generating better initial solutions while preserving the exploratory strengths of RRT-style planners.
