Hi, I am Desiree Fisker, a PhD student in [Mechanical Engineering](https://www.mcgill.ca/mecheng/) at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/) in Montréal, Québec. I work in the [Dynamics, Estimation, and Control in Aerospace and Robotics Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/decarresearch/) with [Prof. James Forbes](https://www.mcgill.ca/mecheng/james-forbes) on collaborative decentralized simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) for robotic systems.

My research focuses on field robotics, state estimation, multi-robot autonomy, UAV and UGV cooperation, perception, scene reconstruction, and GPS-denied navigation. I am especially interested in autonomous systems that work outside controlled lab settings: systems that can share information, build useful maps, and keep navigating when the real world refuses to be tidy.

During my MASc at the [University of Toronto Institute for Aerospace Studies](https://www.utias.utoronto.ca/), I worked in the [Autonomous Space Robotics Lab](https://asrl.utias.utoronto.ca/) with [Prof. Timothy D. Barfoot](https://asrl.utias.utoronto.ca/~tdb/) on [Virtual Teach and Repeat](/projects/virtual-teach-repeat/): a way to use aerial imagery and digital-twin scene reconstructions to virtually teach routes that a ground vehicle can later repeat in the real world.

I have also been shaped by engineering work at [Queen's University](https://www.queensu.ca/), collaboration with [Dr. Melissa Greeff](https://roboralab.com/team.html), and UAV/UGV research in the [Advanced Aircraft Design Lab](https://www.rmc-cmr.ca/en/mechanical-and-aerospace-engineering/ruben-e-perez) at the [Royal Military College of Canada](https://www.rmc-cmr.ca/) with [Dr. Ruben Perez](https://www.rmc-cmr.ca/en/mechanical-and-aerospace-engineering/ruben-e-perez). I am especially drawn to systems that connect careful modeling with practical field testing: building the simulation, running the robot, measuring what actually happened, and iterating until the autonomy becomes useful in messy outdoor environments.

Outside work, my main passions are skydiving and [photography](/projects/photography/). I also love camping, rock climbing, skiing, and scuba diving whenever I can make room for them.
