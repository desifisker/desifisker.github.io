Hi, I am Desiree Fisker, a robotics and aerospace engineer interested in autonomous systems that work outside controlled lab settings.

My research focuses on field robotics, UAV and UGV cooperation, scene reconstruction, perception, and GPS-denied navigation. During my MASc at the University of Toronto Institute for Aerospace Studies, I worked on Virtual Teach and Repeat: a way to use aerial imagery and digital-twin scene reconstructions to virtually teach routes that a ground vehicle can later repeat in the real world.

I am especially drawn to systems that connect careful modeling with practical field testing: building the simulation, running the robot, measuring what actually happened, and iterating until the autonomy becomes useful in messy outdoor environments.
