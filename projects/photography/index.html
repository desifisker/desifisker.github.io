About Me

I enjoy capturing moments in the sky and on the ground, documented with care.

I have been skydiving since 2021 and have worked as a packer, coach, instructor, and tandem video flyer. I have 900 jumps, and I am excited to branch out into event and fun-jumper camera flying so I can capture the stoke and pure joy that comes from all of our favourite moments at the DZ with the ones we love! - Daisy
